Perception data engine for waste-sorting robots

Every robot pick is a free training label.

PickLabel turns a pick's before/after frames, grasp point and gripper feedback into a scored instance mask. It routes the mask into Roboflow as ground truth, review or a hard example, then trains a fast YOLO-seg student that drops into ROS 2 in place of sam3_ros.

Results so far

All measured with the code in the repo on synthetic trays with exact masks. They show correctness and what each design choice is worth. Real-data rows stay empty until the home-rig gold set exists.

Headline: a student trained on PickLabel labels with zero human-labelled instances matched a student trained on 352 ground-truth masks at recall @ precision 0.90 (0.877 vs 0.863; the gap is one item out of 73). It is weaker on mask tightness (AP50-95 0.714 vs 0.771).

How it works

  1. AlignECC with an ORB fallback for a fixed scene; on a belt, the encoder shift is the prior and phase correlation refines it.
  2. Change mapPer-channel gains undo white-balance drift; colour and texture differences take the minimum over small shifts, so nudged neighbours don't count.
  3. Mask at the graspSAM 3 text candidates, else SAM 2.1 prompted with the grasp, the change box and background clicks, else GrabCut.
  4. Score and routeAgreement, change support, contrast and shape give a quality score. Cutoffs are fitted on gold data. Gripper feedback overrides the pixels.
  5. Train and deployLabels are propagated across the session; the split is by session; the YOLO-seg student is judged on recall @ P≥0.90 and ships as a sam3_ros drop-in.
